What makes Wirewatch different

Local App Attribution Brave ↗ :443

Maps active TCP/UDP sockets to local processes (Brave, Cursor, Opera, curl, iCloud) without slow lsof calls, while flagging unsolicited inbound packets with IN ↙.

Per-UID CDN Correlation uid → SNI

Correlates TLS SNI, QUIC, HTTP, and conn.log by Zeek's connection uid first. Two apps talking to different sites on the same Cloudflare/Fastly IP never overwrite each other's domain label.

DoH & Direct-IP Detection [No-DNS/DoH]

Spots when an app or browser connects to a domain via TLS SNI without ever making a local DNS query ([No-DNS/DoH]), or connects to a bare public IP with neither DNS nor SNI ([DIRECT-IP]).

First-Seen Domain Novelty ★ NEW

Remembers registrable base domains across sessions in .wirewatch-known.json and badges brand-new destinations with ★ NEW. Press n or pass --only-new to watch only new/anomalous activity.

Intent & Bonjour Decoder [AI] [TELEM]

Automatically tags [AI], [TELEM], and [OS/SYNC] endpoints and translates cryptic Bonjour/mDNS queries like _companion-link._tcp.local into [Apple Continuity] or [AirPlay].

Volume, Hygiene & Folding [↑ UPLOAD]

Shows ↑sent ↓rcvd bytes on every connection, flags heavy outbound uploads, folds noisy certificate chains into [TLS] (alerting on [EXPIRED-CERT]), and collapses parallel browser socket bursts.

What happens when you run it

  1. Detects your OS & active interface — macOS (Homebrew) or Debian/Ubuntu Linux (apt/Docker).
  2. Installs Zeek automatically if it isn't already on your system.
  3. Creates a clean wirewatch/ folder so Zeek logs and `.wirewatch-known.json` stay contained.
  4. Prompts once for sudo to start packet capture with Zeek's output isolated from your terminal.
  5. Streams enriched traffic until you press q or Ctrl+C, then prints a full session summary.
